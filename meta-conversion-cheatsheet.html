Bonus · Know what you're handing over

What Advantage+ turns on for you.

Advantage+ features let Meta automate parts of the campaign. Useful once you know what you're doing — but each one takes a decision out of your hands. Here's what gets automated so nothing surprises you.

Advantage+ Audience

Meta targets beyond your defined audience whenever it predicts better results.

Advantage+ Placements

Your ad runs across all placements automatically, not just the ones you picked.

Advantage+ Creative

Auto-enhancements — brightness, music, text tweaks, cropping per placement.

Advantage Campaign Budget

Budget shifts freely across ad sets (this is CBO by another name).

Advantage+ Catalog ads

Pulls and rotates products from your catalog dynamically per viewer.

Multi-advertiser ads

Your ad can appear alongside others in a recommendation-style unit.

Scratchpad

Decision tree.

A blank structure to map your own campaign before you build it in Ads Manager. Add a campaign, branch ad sets off it, branch ads off those, and name each one. Nothing here is saved — it's a thinking space.
